# 0.2a — Terms of Use (pilot skeleton, for lawyer review)

**Status:** DRAFT — not for publication. To be reviewed by a lawyer before the first real
provider or PM is onboarded (Decision 5). Operator: the existing OÜ (see decision log).

## 1. Parties and roles

- **Operator** — [OÜ name], registry code [ ], address [ ] ("we").
- **Customer (PM)** — an employee or representative of a construction/fit-out company
  submitting requests for subcontractor capacity.
- **Provider** — a company (OÜ, FIE, AS) offering trade services (the six pilot trades at
  launch) and responding to requests.
- The platform is a **marketplace/venue**: we relay requests, record responses and
  bookings. We are **not a party to any contract** between Customer and Provider, are not
  a labour leasing company, and do not employ or broker the providers' workers.

## 2. Free participation (pilot)

- Provider listing, matching and request response are **free during the pilot**. The
  booking-system interface may become paid after the pilot; price changes are announced
  ≥30 days in advance and never apply retroactively.
- We reserve the right to pause or remove listings that violate these terms.

## 3. Provider obligations

- Accurate, current information: trade(s), service area, pricing model, capacity.
- **Capacity declarations are made in good faith**; the provider confirms availability
  per request and is bound only by an explicit confirmation, not by a declaration alone.
- No fee-charging for listing or responding (providers may not charge us or other
  providers for participation).
- Compliance with tax, labour, and construction-safety law (including valid
  economic-activity notifications where required, e.g. electrical works).

## 4. Booking rules (displayed in-product as well)

- **First confirmed wins:** a booking becomes binding when the Provider confirms and the
  Customer accepts in the platform; the system prevents double-committing the same
  declared capacity *within the platform*.
- **External commitments disclaimer:** the platform cannot know about capacity the
  provider committed elsewhere. Availability marked "Unconfirmed" is an estimate only.
- Holds: a request may hold provider capacity for 48 h while the customer decides
  (feature-flagged; not guaranteed during the pilot).
- Cancellations are recorded and attributed; repeated no-shows may affect the provider's
  reliability display.

## 5. Platform obligations and limitations

- We make no warranty that any request will be matched or any booking completed.
- We do not guarantee the accuracy of provider-declared capacity or the quality of work;
  quality disputes are between Customer and Provider.
- Verification badges reflect registry data at check time (sources and timestamps shown
  in-product) and are not a warranty.

## 6. Data protection

- Personal data is processed per the Privacy Notice (0.2b); worker names and individual
  schedules are **not** collected by the platform — capacity is declared as headcount.
- Both parties keep each other's project information confidential; a customer's project
  details are never shared with providers beyond the anonymised/filtered brief needed to
  respond.

## 7. Liability

- To the maximum extent permitted by law: no indirect damage, lost profit, or data loss
  liability; aggregate liability capped at [€500 / fees paid in prior 12 months — lawyer
  to set]. Estonian law applies; disputes to Harju Maakohus (Tallinn).

## 8. Pilot-specific clauses

- Either party may end pilot participation at any time with written notice.
- We may contact participants for structured feedback (pilot commitment).
- Service level: none guaranteed during pilot ("as is" for availability of the service
  itself).
