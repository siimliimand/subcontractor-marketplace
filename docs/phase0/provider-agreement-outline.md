# 0.2c — Design-Partner / Provider Pilot Agreement — Content Outline

**Status:** DRAFT outline. One agreement template serves both sides (PM design partners
and provider pilot participants) with role-specific annexes. Short-form, pilot-scoped;
full commercial terms come after Gate 3 (monetisation decision).

## Common parts

1. **Parties & term** — pilot until [Gate 3 evaluation / date], either side may exit with
   written notice; no exclusivity.
2. **Free participation** — no fees during pilot (mirrors ToU §2).
3. **Data protection** — reference to Privacy Notice; processing described in
   gdpr-checklist.md §1.
4. **Confidentiality** — each side keeps the other's project/commercial data
   confidential; regulator/registry-public data excluded.
5. **Feedback licence** — participant grants operator a perpetual, free licence to use
   feedback and aggregated (non-identifying) usage data to improve the service.

## Annex A — PM design partner

- **Commitment:** submit ≥[2] real requests during the pilot; respond to structured
  feedback after each (≤15 min); one weekly check-in call.
- **What they get:** priority concierge handling, direct line to the operator, early
  access to response comparison + dashboard views.
- **Concierge honesty:** operator staff may assist in writing briefs and contacting
  providers; every manual step is logged and attributed (transparency about how matches
  were made).
- **No procurement obligation** — they are free to hire off-platform; we ask only to
  record outcomes of platform-initiated matches.

## Annex B — Provider pilot participant

- **Commitment:** keep profile (trades, area, pricing model) current; respond to matched
  requests within 24 h or decline; confirm availability in good faith.
- **Opt-out:** may set "do not contact" at any time; honoured automatically by the
  platform (already modelled in outreach rejections).
- **Listing accuracy** — capacity declarations are estimates, binding only on explicit
  confirmation (mirrors ToU §4).
- **Registry verification:** participant consents to operator checking Äriregister/MTR
  public data for the verification badge; badge shows source + timestamp.
- **Email-first flow:** responses via one-click links (Accept/Quote/Decline) count as
  valid responses under the agreement.

## Open items for the lawyer

1. Feedback-licence clause scope (aggregated usage data definition)
2. Whether the pilot agreement needs a value-in-return clause to be enforceable
   (Estonian contract law: gratuitous promises are weak — the free listing + concierge
   service is the consideration)
3. Signature method: e-signature (Smart-ID later; at pilot, email confirmation recorded
   in audit trail may suffice for B2B — confirm evidentiary standard)
