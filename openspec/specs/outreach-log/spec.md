# Outreach Log Specification

## Purpose
Define how staff log manual outreach batches per provider per request, with guards against duplicate active outreach, opted-out providers, and outreach on booked or cancelled requests.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Log outreach batches
Staff SHALL log an outreach entry per provider per request: batch number, contacted-by staff member, channel (`manual` logged by staff or `matching` generated by a matching run), sent timestamp, optional notes, and — for `matching` entries — the match score and per-factor breakdown that produced the candidate. The uniqueness, opt-out, and stop guards below apply to both channels.

#### Scenario: Log a manual batch
- **WHEN** staff records outreach to 5 providers for a request
- **THEN** 5 outreach entries are stored sharing one batch number, each with its own provider, staff member and timestamp

#### Scenario: Matching-generated entries carry ranking data
- **WHEN** a matching run generates an outreach entry for a provider
- **THEN** the entry is stored with channel `matching` and its match score and factor breakdown are visible wherever the entry is shown

### Requirement: No duplicate active outreach
A provider MUST have at most one active outreach entry per request; adding a second for the same provider-request pair MUST be rejected unless the first is superseded by a wider batch.

#### Scenario: Duplicate provider in a batch
- **WHEN** staff records outreach to a provider already contacted for the same request
- **THEN** the system rejects the duplicate entry and names the existing batch

### Requirement: Opted-out providers excluded
Outreach entries MUST NOT be created for providers whose status is `opted_out`.

#### Scenario: Attempt to contact an opted-out provider
- **WHEN** staff records outreach that includes an opted-out provider
- **THEN** that entry is rejected with the opt-out reason shown, and the other entries in the batch are unaffected

### Requirement: Stop on booked or cancelled
Once a request is `booked` or `cancelled`, no new outreach entries MUST be accepted for it.

#### Scenario: Outreach after booking
- **WHEN** staff attempts to log outreach on a booked request
- **THEN** the system rejects the entry because the request is closed for outreach

### Requirement: Email channel and delivery states on outreach entries
Outreach entries SHALL carry the outreach channel including `email`, and for email
entries a delivery status (`sent`, `delivered`, `bounced`, `opened`, `skipped`) updated
from lifecycle events, so the cockpit shows which providers were contacted and whether
the email reached them.

#### Scenario: Cockpit shows delivery status
- **WHEN** staff views a request's outreach panel after a batch send
- **THEN** each entry shows provider, batch, and its email delivery status with
  timestamps from the audit trail

#### Scenario: Manual outreach unchanged
- **WHEN** staff logs phone or other outreach manually
- **THEN** the entry records `channel='manual'` with no delivery lifecycle, exactly as
  before

### Requirement: Explainability read-back on outreach entries
For outreach entries with channel `matching`, the outreach panel SHALL expose the
stored factor breakdown (per-factor contribution, inputs, missing flags, and the
weights applied at match time) without recomputation.

#### Scenario: Reviewing last week's batch
- **WHEN** staff opens a matching-channel entry created before the current weights
- **THEN** the panel shows the breakdown as stored at match time, including the
  then-active weights
